D&K push key generator

Run this on your own computer. The keys are made inside your browser and are never sent anywhere. Do not put this page on your website and never share the private key.

Public key

Paste into planner/pp-config.js as vapidPublicKey, and into Supabase secret VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY.

Private key

Only for the Supabase secret VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY. Keep it secret.
